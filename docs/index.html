Executive Summary

Bridging Raw Transactions to Actionable Strategy

An enterprise-grade analytical architecture combining automated ETL data cleaning, probabilistic feature engineering, hybrid RFM + unsupervised K-Means segmentation, and relational SQL modeling to drive quantifiable revenue retention.

🧼

Production Data Cleaning

Handles dirty transactional data feeds through automated deduplication, fuzzy category typo matching (difflib), mixed-date ISO coercion, and category-specific IQR outlier detection (Q3 + 1.5×IQR).

🎯

Hybrid Customer Segmentation

Combines rule-based RFM quintile scoring (1–5) mapped to 7 actionable cohorts with unsupervised K-Means clustering (k=4, Silhouette Score: 0.4313) to uncover true behavioral affinity.

📊

Executive BI & Analytics

Powered by a SQLite relational store with Common Table Expressions (CTEs), window functions (RANK(), running totals), and an interactive corporate Streamlit dashboard with real-time dynamic filtering.

Business Context

The Three Core Retail Vulnerabilities

Why traditional retail analytics fall short and how our quantitative framework solves each problem.

⚠️

1. Undifferentiated Mass Marketing

Treating high-value champions and one-time discount seekers identically causes coupon abuse, email fatigue, and squandered marketing spend.

Solution: 7 RFM behavioral cohorts with targeted triggers: VIP concierge for Champions, 15% win-back vouchers for At Risk.
⏱️

2. Silent Customer Churn

Retailers fail to detect when a loyal buyer has exceeded their natural re-order cadence until after they have permanently defected to competitors.

Solution: Dynamic inter-purchase interval tracking flagged 32 high-equity repeat spenders overdue for replenishment (>1.5× cadence).
📉

3. Margin Dilution

Promoting high-GMV hardware categories (Electronics: ₹858.5K, 25.4% margin) without enforcing cross-sell attach rates for high-margin categories (Beauty: 62.8% margin).

Solution: Automated market basket affinity analysis identified high-lift bundles (e.g. Electric Blender + Face Cleanser: 2.39× lift).
Core Architecture

Key Capabilities & Analytical Depth

Built from scratch using industry-standard engineering patterns and rigorous validation.

Data Engineering

Automated Data Cleaning & Quality Audit

A battle-tested 8-step pipeline that ingests messy raw feeds, deduplicates transactions, recomputes corrupted monetary totals, imputes missing values using domain-justified statistical rules, and flags outliers without loss of financial auditability.

  • 75 corrupted records removed (35 duplicate rows, 40 missing primary keys)
  • 4,630 values imputed, normalized, or formatted to strict ISO standards
  • Category-wise IQR fences (Q3 + 1.5×IQR) preserve financial reconciliation
Customer Intelligence

Rule-Based RFM Quintile Scoring

Calculates independent 1–5 quintile scores across Recency (days since last purchase), Frequency (order count), and Monetary value (total spend). Translates composite RFM vectors into 7 distinct behavioral cohorts.

  • Champions (32 accounts): 56.2% of company revenue, avg 18.9 orders
  • Loyal Customers (26 accounts): 21.3% of revenue, avg 10.0 orders
  • Can't Lose Them (11 accounts): 9.7% revenue share, 73 days dormant
Machine Learning

Unsupervised K-Means Segmentation

Standardizes customer features with StandardScaler and evaluates optimal cluster counts using Elbow Inertia curvature and Silhouette Score maximization. Identified mathematically distinct clusters with an impressive 0.4313 silhouette score.

  • Cluster 0: Core VIP Champions (42 buyers, ₹64.3K avg spend, 14 days recency)
  • Cluster 1: Dormant Low-Frequency (36 buyers, ₹4.9K avg spend, 188 days recency)
  • Cluster 2: Steady Mid-Tier Spenders (28 buyers, ₹18.5K avg spend, 32 days recency)
Financial Analytics

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Modeling

Calculates customer equity using historical transaction velocity, average basket size, and an empirical 3.0-year conservative retail lifespan horizon: CLV = AOV × Purchase Frequency × Lifespan.

  • Average Portfolio CLV: ₹87,702.48
  • Top Customer CLV: Shreya Sharma (C0079) at ₹479,077.74
  • Repeat Buyer CLV Premium: 13.1× greater lifetime spend than one-time shoppers
Relational Database

Production SQL Analytics Engine

Syncs processed transactions and customer cohorts into a high-performance SQLite database (retail.db). Executes 8 production-grade SQL analytical queries featuring CTEs, window functions, and cross-checks against Pandas metrics.

  • Window Functions: RANK() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC)
  • Running Totals: SUM(...) OVER (PARTITION BY ... ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING...)
  • Automated integration tests verify 100% numerical parity with Pandas

Methodology

7-Stage End-to-End Analytics Workflow

A completely modular, orchestrated data pipeline executed via src/run_pipeline.py, make pipeline, or ./run.sh pipeline.

1

Data Generation & Defect Injection

src/generate_data.py

Synthesizes 1,235 realistic transactions for 129 customers across 11 Indian cities and 6 categories, intentionally injecting real-world defects: duplicate rows, casing typos, mixed date formats, null keys, and bulk outliers.

2

Modular Data Cleaning & Quality Audit

src/clean_data.py

Executes 8 deterministic cleaning routines: removes 35 duplicates, coerces ISO dates, fixes 97 typos with fuzzy matching, drops 40 null keys, imputes age via median, recomputes monetary totals, and logs full audit metrics to reports/cleaning_log.md.

3

Feature Engineering (Transaction & Customer 360°)

src/features.py

Generates 36 transaction-level features (COGS, gross profit, margin %, festive season flags) and 26 customer-level features (AOV, purchase frequency, recency, average inter-purchase days, 3-year CLV).

4

Hybrid Customer Segmentation

src/segmentation.py

Computes quintile-based RFM scores (1–5) and maps them to 7 strategic cohorts. Runs K-Means clustering (k=4), generates Elbow and Silhouette diagnostic charts, and assigns tailored marketing actions.

5

Relational SQLite Modeling & SQL Queries

src/load_db.py & sql/queries.sql

Loads 1,160 clean transactions and 129 customer records into SQLite (data/processed/retail.db), creates search indexes, and verifies 8 CTE analytical queries with window functions against Pandas metrics.

6

Automated Insights Synthesis & PDF Compilation

src/insights.py

Dynamically computes 9 strategic business insights and compiles executive reports in both Markdown (reports/Project_Report.md) and publication-quality PDF (reports/Project_Report.pdf).

7

Interactive Streamlit Executive Dashboard

dashboard/app.py

Launches an interactive corporate BI dashboard powered by Plotly, offering real-time cohort exploration, margin vs. volume toggles, cross-sell matrices, and segment drill-downs.

Quantitative Insights

Key Strategic Business Findings

Extracted directly from the computed analytics in reports/insights.md and verified against SQLite.

Revenue Concentration 👑

Pareto 80/20 Distribution

40.3% → 80%

Top 20% of customers (26 accounts) drive 57.29% of revenue. Just 52 customers (40.3%) generate 80.0% of total sales. Top buyer Tanvi Sharma alone generated ₹286,439.42 across 26 orders.

Strategic Action: Establish a VIP Concierge Tier with dedicated account management and high-tier anniversary rewards.
Retention Equity 🔄

13.1× Repeat Multiplier

13.1× Spend

Repeat buyers average ₹32,752.20 in lifetime spend vs. only ₹2,497.05 for one-time buyers. Repeat shoppers represent 88.37% of customers and 99.01% of total revenue.

Strategic Action: Deploy an automated 21-day "Second Order Bridge" nurture sequence offering a 10% voucher on complementary categories.
Margin Architecture 💄

Beauty vs. Electronics Margins

62.8% Margin

Electronics drives high GMV (₹858.5K) but delivers the lowest margin (25.43%). In contrast, Beauty delivers the highest margin (62.81%) and Clothing drives ₹402.5K in absolute profit.

Strategic Action: Mandate checkout attach-rate bundles pairing high-margin beauty and accessories with electronics orders.
Geographic Optimization 📍

North Volume vs. East AOV

₹3,860 East AOV

The North Region leads total revenue with ₹1,068,592.56 (28.3% share across 332 orders). However, the East Region commands the highest Average Order Value at ₹3,860.22 (+19.9% vs North).

Strategic Action: Optimize North logistics for fulfillment speed, while targeting East hubs with high-ticket bundle promotions.
Churn Prevention 🚨

Latent Churn Risk

32 Overdue

32 repeat customers have exceeded 1.5× their historical purchase cadence. High-equity accounts like Shreya Sharma (CLV: ₹479.1K) are overdue, representing ₹476,000+ in salvageable retention value.

Strategic Action: Automate personalized "We Miss You" SMS and email win-back sequences triggered at 1.25× personal purchase interval.
Market Basket Affinity 🛍️

Cross-Selling Lift

2.39× Lift

Customers purchasing an Electric Blender are 2.39× more likely to buy a Face Cleanser (13 co-purchases), and 2.24× more likely to co-purchase a Power Bank.

Strategic Action: Program one-click cart cross-sell popups pairing lifestyle essentials with kitchen appliances.
